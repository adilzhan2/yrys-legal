Nisab 2027 (1448 AH)

How much you need before zakat is due, in dollars, pounds, rupees and other currencies, and how to get the exact figure on your zakat date.

Yrys guide · 4 min read · prices as of 18 November 2026

The nisab is the minimum amount on which zakat is due: the value of 85 g of gold or 595 g of silver. If your cash, gold, silver and business goods minus debts stay at or above the nisab for a full lunar year, you give 2.5%.

Calculate nisab and zakat

Gold or silver?

The difference is large: the silver threshold is currently about 12 times lower than the gold one. All four schools accept both thresholds for gold and silver themselves. They differ on how to measure money:

  • Silver (595 g). The Hanafi view: of the two thresholds, use the one that is better for the poor. More people pay zakat.
  • Gold (85 g). The view of many contemporary scholars (Yusuf al-Qaradawi, the AAOIFI standard): silver has lost so much value that its threshold no longer reflects real wealth.

If you follow the Hanafi school or are unsure, silver is the safer choice. Many mosques and national zakat bodies publish their own nisab figure each year, and you can follow theirs.

How to work out the nisab yourself

Gold nisab = 85 × gold price per gram today
Silver nisab = 595 × silver price per gram today

Use the market price per gram on the day you calculate your zakat. On 18 November 2026, for example, gold was about $122.50 per gram, so the gold nisab was 85 × $122.50 ≈ $10,400.

Some books give the nisab as 87.48 g of gold and 612.36 g of silver. These are the same 20 mithqal and 200 dirhams, converted to grams differently. The difference is small; the calculator uses 85 and 595 g.

When to compare with the nisab

Zakat is due if your wealth stays at or above the nisab for a full lunar year, about 354 days. It helps to pick one date and calculate on it every year.

  • Hanafi school: what matters is having the nisab at the start and end of the year; a short dip below it in between doesn’t break the year.
  • Other schools: your wealth must stay at or above the nisab throughout the year.

Ramadan 2027

The year 1448 AH began in summer 2026. Ramadan 1448 is expected to begin around 8 February 2027, and Eid al-Fitr around 9–10 March; your local moon-sighting authority will confirm the dates. Many people give their zakat in Ramadan, when the reward for good deeds is greater.

If your zakat date is later but you want to give in Ramadan, you can: most scholars allow paying zakat early if you already have the nisab. Work it out at the prices on the day you pay.

Don’t confuse zakat with zakat al-fitr: it is given for each member of the family at the end of Ramadan, before the Eid prayer. More in the guide “Zakat vs sadaqah”.
