Yrys

A fast, private spending tracker for iPhone, with a quiet check on impulse buys. Everything stays on your device.

Yrys has no servers, no account and no sign-in. Your accounts, categories and transactions live only on your iPhone. The optional Yrys Pro subscription is sold through the App Store.

Common questions

How do I cancel Yrys Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Yrys and tap Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for. Refund requests go to Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, open Menu → Erase All Data. To remove everything, including the app's local safety copies, delete the app from your iPhone. We never hold a copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Does Yrys sync between devices?

No. Yrys works offline and does not sync. Your data is included in your iPhone's standard iCloud or computer backup, and you can export it as a CSV file from the Menu at any time.
