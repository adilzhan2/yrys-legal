Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

  • Yrys works entirely on your iPhone. It has no servers, no account and no sign-in.
  • We collect no personal data. There are no analytics, ads, cookies or tracking of any kind.
  • Your data leaves the device only when you choose: when you export a file, or as part of your own iOS backup.

Yrys is a personal spending log for iPhone. It works fully offline. This policy explains what the app stores, where that information lives and how you can delete it.

Who we are

Yrys is developed and published by an individual developer, listed as the seller on the App Store page for Yrys. For anything related to this policy, write to НОВЫЙ@АДРЕС.

What data we collect

None. Yrys has no account or sign-in. It does not ask for your contacts, location, photos or device identifiers. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it makes no network requests of its own.

Where your data lives

Your accounts, categories and transactions are stored only on your device, inside the app. We have no access to them and keep no copy. This information reaches iCloud only as part of your device's standard iOS backup, which you control in your iPhone's iCloud settings and which is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Quiz

If you take the short quiz when you first open the app, your answers are stored only on your device and used to show your result and tailor what the app highlights. They are never sent anywhere. You can skip the quiz at any step.

Purchases

Yrys Pro is sold through the App Store as an auto-renewable subscription. Apple processes the payment and your payment details under Apple's own privacy policy. Neither the app nor we receive your name, email address or payment details. The app stores only your purchase status and the subscription end date on your device, so Pro keeps working offline. In App Store Connect we see only anonymous, aggregated sales reports.

Statistics from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymous, aggregated statistics with us, such as the number of installs or crashes. The app itself collects and sends nothing.

Export and import

When you ask, the app creates a CSV file with your transactions and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where that file goes next is up to you. Importing a file works the other way round: the app reads a file you pick and nothing is uploaded.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is a local notification created on your device. It does not go through any server, and you can turn it off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Deleting your data

Menu → Erase All Data deletes your accounts, categories and transactions. Before erasing, the app saves a safety copy on the same device so the step can be undone. To delete everything, including those copies, delete the app from your iPhone. Copies inside iOS backups are removed together with those backups, in your iCloud settings or on your computer.

Your rights

We do not hold any personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. All your data is on your device, under your control. If you have a question or a complaint, write to НОВЫЙ@АДРЕС. You can also contact the data protection authority where you live; in the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office, in Canada the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Children

Yrys is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts to work differently, we will update this page first. New versions are published at this address with a new effective date.
